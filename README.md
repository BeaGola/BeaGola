# Hi, I'm Beatrice 👋

I'm a product person at heart - and currently making the leap from product leader to founder.

For the past 15+ years, I've been building digital products, shaping product strategies, working with teams and organizations, and asking probably way too many questions about why customers do what they do.

I love messy problems.

The kind where it's not yet clear what the product should be.  
Or whether there should even be a product at all.

That's usually where I get curious.

## What I'm up to these days 🔎

Right now, I'm spending a lot of time exploring new business ideas, talking to customers, testing assumptions and figuring out which problems are actually worth building a company around.

AI has made that even more interesting.

Not because I believe everything suddenly needs an AI feature — but because it allows people like me to go much further from **idea → experiment → actual product** than was possible just a few years ago.

So this GitHub is partly my playground.

You'll find experiments, prototypes, things I'm learning, things I'm building and probably a few things that don't work yet.

That's kind of the point. :)
